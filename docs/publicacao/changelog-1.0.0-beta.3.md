# Arraial 1.0.0-beta.3

**Important fix / Correção importante:** several Arraial recipes took the place of vanilla ones. The worst case: the Marco da Obra replaced the **wooden pickaxe**. All recipes were redone and checked against the game, so none of them clash any more. / Algumas receitas do Arraial tomavam o lugar de receitas do jogo (o Marco da Obra no lugar da **picareta de madeira**). Todas foram refeitas e conferidas, e nenhuma conflita mais.
- Marco da Obra: planks + stick + paper (shapeless) / tábua + graveto + papel (sem forma)
- Cuia: pumpkin + bowl / abóbora + tigela
- Réis: copper or gold ingot + paper / lingote de cobre ou ouro + papel
- Telha: 2 bricks + clay ball / 2 tijolos + bola de argila
- Cruzeiro: **any kind of log** / **qualquer tora**

**New / Novidade: villages founded before your eyes / vilas fundadas na sua frente.** While you explore, a caravan of settlers with a laden donkey walks in from the horizon. They plant the Cruzeiro, light a campfire and unload the donkey into the armazém, and their builder raises the first house, the roça and the well block by block. From then on the village grows on its own. You can switch this off in the settings, and `/arraial colonos` calls a caravan for videos. / Enquanto você explora, uma caravana de colonos com um burro carregado chega, finca o Cruzeiro, acende uma fogueira e o pedreiro levanta a primeira casa, a roça e o poço bloco a bloco.

**Integrations / Integrações** (all optional / todas opcionais):
- **Jade:** look at a morador to see their trade, village, what they are doing, family, mood, hunger and sickness. Look at the Cruzeiro to see the stage, era, population, satisfaction, treasury, mayor and crisis. Look at a workstation to see who works there.
- **Xaero's Minimap / World Map:** each village you have walked into becomes a waypoint, in its flag colour. Abandoned villages are marked with ☠.
- **Mod Menu:** a settings screen with the following options:
  - how close neighbouring villages appear, and how many of them;
  - the share of Spanish missions;
  - whether bases start occupied;
  - whether crises happen, and how often;
  - how fast newcomers arrive;
  - whether soldiers take armour from the storehouse;
  - the border notice;
  - map waypoints.
  
  Without Mod Menu, edit `config/arraial.json`.
- **REI (Roughly Enough Items):** the same information pages as JEI.
- **Farmer's Delight Refabricated:**
  - three Brazilian dishes for the cooking pot: **baião de dois**, **moqueca** and **vaca atolada**;
  - the peixeira works as a knife on the cutting board;
  - farmers also plant cabbage and onion if you put them in the storehouse.
- **Terralith:** all Terralith biomes are recognised by the region map and by village cultures.
