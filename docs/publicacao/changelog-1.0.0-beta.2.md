# Arraial 1.0.0-beta.2

**Important fix / Correção importante:** on natural grass, builders could not find a place for any building (Minecraft 26.3 no longer counts grass as dirt). Villages on normal terrain now grow as they should. / Em terreno natural com grama, os pedreiros não achavam lugar para obra nenhuma; agora as vilas crescem normalmente.

**New / Novidades** (suggested by the community / sugeridas pela comunidade):
- **Fazendas:** sugar mill (engenho) with a sugar master: cane, sugar and rapadura. Coffee plantation (cafezal) with a coffee grower: coffee bushes, roasted coffee and the cafezinho drink (haste). The herder makes queijo coalho from the corral's milk and charque from spare beef.
- **Brazilian Baroque:** soapstone and gilded carving blocks, the carver and the Igreja Matriz with two bell towers. Mining towns build white houses on soapstone from the Imperial era.
- **Spanish missions:** about a third of neighbouring villages are Spanish missions, with Spanish names, the cross of Burgundy, adobe walls and a mission church with a bell-gable. Tension rises more easily on the frontier.
- **Armour from other mods:** soldiers wear and wield the best armour and weapon in the storehouse, from any mod.
- 3 new trades, 4 new advancements, 5 new pages in the Guia, new villager skins for the new trades.

**Fixes / Correções:** wooden pews no longer turn into roof tiles in the Imperial era; ground search tolerates gentler slopes.
