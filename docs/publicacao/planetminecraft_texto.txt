Found an arraial, hire a builder, and watch a Brazilian colonial village grow on its own: from a handful of wattle-and-daub huts around a wooden cross to a whitewashed Cidade with a chapel, a fountain, a town council, and neighbours to trade with, or go to war with.

VILLAGES THAT LIVE

- 16 trades, each with its own workstation and look: builder, woodcutter, farmer, potter, clay digger, quarryman, vaqueiro, fisher, hunter, flour maker, stallholder, muleteer, apothecary, soldier, engineer and envoy.
- Real construction: pick a project and the builders raise it block by block with materials from the storehouse. The mayor orders new works by himself.
- Stages: Povoado -> Arraial -> Vila -> Prosperous Vila -> Cidade.
- People, not mobs: personalities, friendships, rivalries, favours, weddings, children and inheritance.

ECONOMY AND POLITICS

- Réis, a market stall with supply-and-demand prices, peddlers, orders, muleteers between villages, beaten roads and supply contracts.
- A council of interest groups, proposals and votes, taxes, corruption, protests and hereditary coronéis.

A LIVING REGION

- Neighbouring villages grow, migrate, trade, quarrel over land and go to war, even while you are away.
- Chunk-based territory, border markers, disputes, skirmishes and peace with tribute.
- The Chancery: a diplomacy screen with a regional map, where you propose treaties, demand tribute or declare embargoes.
- Abandoned villages fall into ruin, and bandits move in.

CULTURE

- Festas: Saint John's bonfire and quadrilha, the patron saint, the Great Fair, and each village's own traditions.
- Crises: drought, flood, pests, highwaymen, epidemics and fire.

THREE ERAS
Colonial (taipa) -> Imperial (brick and clay tiles) -> Republican (lime and azulejo). Builders reform houses and streets in each era's style.

ITEMS
Machete, peixeira, sickle, vaqueiro leather, a flask, Brazilian foods (farinha, beiju, paçoca, rapadura, pé-de-moleque, quentão) and colonial blocks.

GETTING STARTED
Craft a Cruzeiro (5 dark oak logs in a cross) and place it: you get the Guia do Arraial, a 49-page in-game manual whose first chapter walks you through your first days.

REQUIREMENTS
Minecraft 26.3 | Fabric Loader 0.20.1+ | Fabric API (https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) | Java 25 | optional: JEI. Needed on both client and server. Fully translated into English and Portuguese (Brazil).
