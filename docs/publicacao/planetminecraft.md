## Descrição (BBCode)

```
[b]⚠ Beta:[/b] back up your worlds and report bugs on [url=https://github.com/Dants0/arraial/issues]GitHub[/url].

[b]Found an arraial, hire a builder, and watch a Brazilian colonial village grow on its own[/b]: from a handful of wattle-and-daub huts around a wooden cross to a whitewashed Cidade with a chapel, a fountain, a town council, and neighbours to trade with, or go to war with.

[b]🏘 VILLAGES THAT LIVE[/b]
[list]
[*][b]16 trades[/b], each with its own workstation and look: builder, woodcutter, farmer, potter, clay digger, quarryman, vaqueiro, fisher, hunter, flour maker, stallholder, muleteer, apothecary, soldier, engineer and envoy.
[*][b]Real construction[/b]: pick a project and the builders raise it block by block with materials from the storehouse. The mayor orders new works by himself.
[*][b]Stages[/b]: Povoado → Arraial → Vila → Prosperous Vila → Cidade.
[*][b]People, not mobs[/b]: personalities, friendships, rivalries, favours, weddings, children and inheritance.
[/list]

[b]💰 ECONOMY AND POLITICS[/b]
[list]
[*]Réis, a market stall with supply-and-demand prices, peddlers, orders, muleteers between villages, beaten roads and supply contracts.
[*]A council of interest groups, proposals and votes, taxes, corruption, protests and hereditary coronéis.
[/list]

[b]🗺 A LIVING REGION[/b]
[list]
[*]Neighbouring villages grow, migrate, trade, quarrel over land and go to war, even while you are away.
[*]Chunk-based territory, border markers, disputes, skirmishes and peace with tribute.
[*][b]The Chancery[/b]: a diplomacy screen with a regional map, where you propose treaties, demand tribute or declare embargoes.
[*]Abandoned villages fall into ruin, and bandits move in.
[/list]

[b]🎉 CULTURE[/b]
[list]
[*]Festas: Saint John's bonfire and quadrilha, the patron saint, the Great Fair, and each village's own traditions.
[*]Crises: drought, flood, pests, highwaymen, epidemics and fire.
[/list]

[b]🧱 THREE ERAS[/b]
Colonial (taipa) → Imperial (brick and clay tiles) → Republican (lime and azulejo). Builders reform houses and streets in each era's style.

[b]🎒 ITEMS[/b]
Machete, peixeira, sickle, vaqueiro leather, a flask, Brazilian foods (farinha, beiju, paçoca, rapadura, pé-de-moleque, quentão) and colonial blocks.

[b]📖 GETTING STARTED[/b]
Craft a [b]Cruzeiro[/b] (5 dark oak logs in a cross) and place it: you get the [b]Guia do Arraial[/b], a 49-page in-game manual whose first chapter walks you through your first days.

[b]REQUIREMENTS[/b]
Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric Loader 0.20.1+ · [url=https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api]Fabric API[/url] · Java 25 · optional: JEI. Needed on both client and server. Fully translated into English and Portuguese (Brazil).

[b]LINKS[/b]
[url=https://github.com/Dants0/arraial]Source and downloads (GitHub)[/url] · [url=https://dantas-dev.com/en/blog]Devblog (EN)[/url] · [url=https://dantas-dev.com/pt/blog]Devblog (PT)[/url]
```
