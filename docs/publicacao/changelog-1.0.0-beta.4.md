# Arraial 1.0.0-beta.4

**New / Novidade: Brazilian wildlife / fauna brasileira.** Five animals now live in the warm biomes. / Cinco bichos agora vivem nos biomas quentes:
- **Capybara / Capivara:** lives in groups by rivers, lakes and swamps. It is tame and swims well. Breed it with melon, sugar cane or wheat; it drops leather and meat. / em bando na beira d'água. Mansa, nada bem; cria-se com melancia, cana ou trigo. Dá couro e carne.
- **Giant anteater / Tamanduá-bandeira:** walks slowly and alone through savannas and plains. Harmless; breed it with berries. / sozinho no cerrado e nas planícies. Inofensivo; cria-se com frutas silvestres.
- **Caiman / Jacaré:** lurks at the water's edge in swamps and rivers. On dry land it leaves you alone, but it bites whoever comes close to the bank and snatches chickens and young capybaras. Breed it with fish. / espreita na beira de rios e brejos. Morde quem chega na margem e pega galinhas e filhotes de capivara.
- **Jaguar / Onça-pintada:** lives in jungles, and rarely in savannas. It slips away from people unless attacked. Now and then it hunts capybaras, chickens, pigs and sheep, corral animals included. / foge de gente, mas caça capivaras e bichos do curral. Vila perto da mata quer soldados.
- **Macaw / Arara:** comes in red, blue-and-yellow and hyacinth, in jungles and savannas. It flies, perches and dances like a parrot and can be tamed with seeds. / vermelha, canindé ou azul; voa e dança como papagaio e doma-se com sementes.

Spawn eggs are in the creative tab, with JEI/REI pages. The guide book has a new **Fauna** chapter. / Ovos geradores na aba criativa, com páginas no JEI/REI. O Guia ganhou o capítulo **Fauna**.

**Villages / Vilas:**
- A new village that lacks a trade nobody can take up now **improvises the workstation**. For example, with no woodcutter there were no planks, so nothing got built. Now the mayor sets down a chopping block and someone becomes a woodcutter. / Quando falta um ofício que ninguém pode assumir, o prefeito **improvisa o posto** e alguém assume o ofício. Antes, por exemplo, sem lenhador não havia tábua e nada era construído.
- `/arraial diagnostico`: a one-line summary of the nearest village, covering:
  - people, beds, homeless, hungry, sick;
  - food, satisfaction, treasury, crisis;
  - works done and in progress, trades;
  - the current request.
